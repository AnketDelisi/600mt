Method: Senate races start from the state's lean in the last presidential election, adjusted for the national environment (scaled by the state's historical elasticity), incumbency and trend. House races start from the district's last presidential margin (The Downballot, 2026 lines), the last House result and the rating band. Governor races start from the state's last presidential lean, the rating band and incumbency. Polls are rebuilt from the individual surveys with recency and partisan weighting and an outlier guard, then blended. The Monte Carlo runs 20,000 nights with a national swing, a correlated per-state deviation and a cycle poll-bias term. Races without public polling get their probability from the fundamentals alone. Sources: Wikipedia (2026 Senate, House and gubernatorial election articles and their ratings tables), The Downballot's district presidential results, and the generic-ballot aggregates. The map painter on the map builder uses the same boundaries as the forecast map.
