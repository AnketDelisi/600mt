Method: Senate races start from the state's lean in the last presidential election, adjusted for the national environment (scaled by the state's historical elasticity), incumbency and trend. House and governor races start from district/state PVI, the last result and the rating band. Polls are rebuilt from the individual surveys with recency and partisan weighting and an outlier guard, then blended. The Monte Carlo runs 20,000 nights with a national swing and a correlated per-state deviation. Races without public polling get their probability from the fundamentals alone. Sources: Wikipedia (2026 Senate, House and gubernatorial election articles and their ratings tables) and the generic-ballot aggregates. The map painter on the map builder uses the same boundaries as the forecast map.
