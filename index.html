Method: the 600 model starts from district/state partisan lean (PVI and the last election result), clamps that margin inside the race-rating band (Cook, Inside Elections, Sabato), blends in per-race polling averages where available, then runs a national-swing Monte Carlo centered on the generic ballot. Races without public polling get their probability from the rating and lean alone. Sources: Wikipedia (2026 Senate, House and gubernatorial election articles and their ratings tables) and the generic-ballot aggregates.
